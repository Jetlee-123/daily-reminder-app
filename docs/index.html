Privacy Policy for Daily Reminder

Last updated: September 2026

Daily Reminder ("the app") is a task reminder application developed for Android. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Summary

Daily Reminder does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter into the app — task titles, notes, checklists, tags, reminder times, and settings — is stored locally on your device only, using a local database. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and the app does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Data the app does not collect

  • No personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or crash reporting sent off-device
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No contacts, photos, or files from your device

Permissions the app requests, and why

  • Notifications — required to show you your task reminders.
  • Exact alarms — required so reminders fire at the precise time you set, rather than an approximate time.
  • Receive boot completed — required so your scheduled reminders continue to work after your device restarts.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data — they only allow the app to function as intended, entirely on your device.

Data deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the app's storage in your device's Settings → Apps menu.

Children's privacy

Daily Reminder does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from any user at all.

Changes to this policy

If this app ever adds features that involve data collection or cloud sync in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect that before such features are released, and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
